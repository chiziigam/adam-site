02

What Adam hears

Adam listens for the moments that matter.

  1. A fall

    Adam asks if they are okay. With no answer in about 30 seconds it tells the family, and if nobody responds, it calls 999. Saying “I’m fine” stops it at any point.

  2. The smoke or CO alarm

    It tells whoever is home, wherever they are in the house, day or night.

  3. A baby crying

    It lets the parent on duty know, even with the telly on downstairs.

  4. A knock at the door

    It tells you, even when you are out in the garden.

  5. Glass breaking

    It alerts you straight away.

It knows who is in which room, so the message reaches the right person.

03

Privacy and safety

What Adam does on its own, and what waits for you.

Adam hears the room so it can act in an emergency. Here is exactly where that sound goes and what Adam is allowed to do with it.

Stays in your home
The sound itself. Audio never leaves the house, and neither does what Adam knows about your household.
Goes online
Only the text of your words, when you ask Adam something, so your assistant can answer.
Not in the box
Cameras. Nothing in Adam can see. It knows which room someone is in, never their exact position.
Happens without asking
After a fall with no answer in about 30 seconds, Adam tells the family, then calls 999 if nobody responds. Smoke and CO alarms reach whoever is home. Saying “I’m fine” stops a fall alert at any point.
Waits for your OK
Paying, ordering, booking and sending messages or emails. Anything that can’t be undone needs your approval first.
Yours to control
See, export or delete everything Adam keeps.

If Wi-Fi drops, Adam carries on through your phone’s hotspot. If both are down, it sounds an alarm out loud.
