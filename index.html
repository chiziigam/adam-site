Safety built in.

Safety is one of the things Adam does, not the reason it exists. You do not set it up in an app. If something happens at home, Adam hears it and tells the right person.

  1. A fall

    Adam asks if they are okay. With no answer in about 30 seconds it tells the family, and if nobody responds, it can get emergency help. Saying “I’m fine” stops it at any point.

  2. The smoke or CO alarm

    It tells whoever is home, wherever they are in the house, day or night.

  3. A baby crying

    It lets the parent on duty know, even with the telly on downstairs.

  4. A knock at the door

    It tells you, even when you are out in the garden.

  5. Glass breaking

    It alerts you straight away.

It knows who is in which room, so the message reaches the right person.
